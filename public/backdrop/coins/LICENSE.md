# Hero coin sprites

`gold-*.png`, `silver-*.png` and `bimetal-*.png` are generated by
`tmp/make-coins.mjs` — original artwork, no third-party assets, drawn in
SVG and rasterized at 640px with headless Chromium. Regenerate with:

    node tmp/make-coins.mjs

The designs are generic (a € mark, twelve stars, a milled rim) and are not
reproductions of any issuing authority's coin artwork.
